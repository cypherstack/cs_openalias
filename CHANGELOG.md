## 0.0.1

- First release.
- One call, `resolveOpenAlias`, for `oa1:<application>` records with a
  required application tag, plus `isOpenAlias` and
  `isOpenAliasApplication`.
- DNS over HTTPS with on-device DNSSEC validation, direct or through SOCKS5
  with Tor isolation.
- Six `CsOpenAliasException` leaves.
